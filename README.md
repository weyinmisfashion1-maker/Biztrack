# BizTrack.ng — Nigerian SME Business Manager

BizTrack.ng is a cloud-powered management tool designed specifically for Nigerian small business owners. Track your daily sales, manage expenses, monitor inventory, and generate professional invoices—all in one place.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Daily Sales Recording:** Log transactions with customer details and delivery tracking.
- **Expense Management:** Keep a sharp eye on your costs and overheads.
- **Stock Tracking:** Real-time inventory levels to prevent stockouts.
- **Professional Invoices:** Generate PNG or PDF invoices with your business logo and bank details.
- **FIRS Tax Estimation:** Automatic estimates based on Nigerian SME tax rates.
- **Cloud Powered:** Data is securely synced to Supabase, accessible on both Web and Android.
